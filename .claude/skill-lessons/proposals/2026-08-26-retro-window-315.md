## P1 — pr-harden: raising the round cap is a third move, and the signal that licenses it

**Target:** `pr-harden` §Termination, the one-sentence cap paragraph (`pr-harden:529`).

**Bar met: clause 1 — two run records.**
- `#308`: "the default cap of 4 was reached with a real, one-case finding outstanding. Raised to 6
  explicitly rather than ending as did-not-converge over a coverage gap; rounds 5 and 6 then converged.
  **Every round 1-5 found a genuinely DIFFERENT defect**, which is the signal that distinguished 'loop
  working' from 'loop spinning'."
- `#315`: "the round cap (4) was reached mid-convergence with one-line findings outstanding. Extended
  twice (to 5, then 6) rather than declaring did-not-converge; **findings shrank monotonically**, so the
  extensions were right, but the skill offers no guidance on when extending beats overriding."

`REJECTED.md` parks this at "**1 record** (#308). Saved rounds rather than costing them, so below the
bar on both limbs." `#315` moves the record limb to two. The cost limb is still NOT met and the
proposal does not claim it: neither run lost a round. What the absence costs is the terminus, not
rounds — the skill as written offers the cap only as an override, so a run that obeys it ends two
demonstrably-converging loops as *did not converge* with one-line findings outstanding.

Both records independently name a discriminator, and they are the same shape: evidence the loop is
still working (a different defect each round; findings shrinking) rather than spinning.

**Proposed edit.** Before:

> The **round cap** (default 4) is the other. A cap is not convergence; reaching it is an override.

After:

> The **round cap** (default 4) is the other. A cap is not convergence; reaching it is an override.
> **Raising it is a third move, and twice it has been the right one** — the cap reached with a small
> real finding outstanding, raised, and the next rounds converged. What licenses a raise is evidence
> the loop is still WORKING rather than spinning: each round finding a DIFFERENT defect, or findings
> shrinking round on round. A round that re-raises what an earlier one raised, or findings holding
> their size, is spinning — take the override instead. Say which signal you read and what you raised
> the cap to; a raise nobody states turns a *did not converge* into a *converged* silently.

**Step 4.** Retires nothing. Net growth ~6 lines. Justification: it is the only place in either
convergence skill where a move two runs made, and both got right, has no rule — and the rule it does
have points the other way. Paid for by the prune under P2.

**Not proposed:** any change to `resolve-ticket:62` or `ticket-pool:198`, both of which say the cap can
end a run without convergence. That stays true; a raise is a move *before* the terminus, not a new one.
Widening a second skill is a second proposal.

## P4 — harden: a repeat is evidence only where something between the repeats is reset

**Target:** `harden:62-63`, the closing sentence of the stand-in/input-population paragraph.

**Bar met: clause 2 — one record, two rounds.** `#315`, Refuted by measurement: "'n=3 byte-identical
repeats establish stability' -> consecutive repeats measure KV-CACHE stability, not answer stability
(LocalLlmEngine's own javadoc: cache_prompt makes a borderline argmax non-deterministic). **Every prompt
figure in the first 5 harden cycles was weaker than it looked** · cost: 2 rounds". And `[r2]`: "the fix
does not resolve the ticket on the ticket's own arrangement, 12/13 interleaved — and **the n=3
methodology is structurally blind to it**" · blocking · cost: 2 rounds.

**Why here.** This is the same shape as the two paragraphs above it, one step further out: the paragraph
already asks what a STUB could not express, then what an INPUT POPULATION could not contain. The next
stand-in is the METHOD — repeats that could not have varied. Mirroring the closest sibling puts it in
that paragraph rather than in a new home.

**Proposed edit.** Append to the existing sentence "Ask of any clean or zero result what its inputs
could not have produced, and ask it of a measurement you rely on as readily as one you ran.":

> **And of a STABLE result, ask what the repeats could not have varied.** On the #315 run, n=3
> byte-identical answers were published as stability while every repeat reused the server's cached
> prefix — the engine's own javadoc says that cache makes a borderline argmax non-deterministic, so the
> repeats measured the cache, and every figure from five cycles was weaker than it looked. Name what is
> reset between repeats; where nothing is, n repeats are one sample.

**Step 4.** Net growth ~4 lines, retires nothing. Justification: it is the third instance of a shape the
paragraph already teaches twice, and the run it comes from had five cycles of figures resting on it.

**Known weakness a refuter should press:** the mechanism cited (`cache_prompt`) is specific to one
project's engine, so this may belong in that repo's `CLAUDE.md` rather than in a governance skill. The
generalisation is stated first and the citation is evidence, which is the house form — but the refuter
should decide whether the class (a warm cache, a memoised value, a persisted marker between repeats) is
general enough to earn skill-level text.
