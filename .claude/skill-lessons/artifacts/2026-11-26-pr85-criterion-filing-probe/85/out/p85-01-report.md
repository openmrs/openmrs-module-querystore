**Set aside**
- **R1.** The six helpers take a value the caller has already read through `AdministrationService`. Core 2.8.0's core data grants "Get Global Properties" to the Authenticated role, and `UserContext.getAllRoles` gives every logged-in user that role (checked with `javap` on the 2.8.0 jar).
